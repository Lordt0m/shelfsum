**Blocked by:** 04: Maintain and find Products; 05: Add Staff Members with enforced permissions; 08: Void completed stock documents; 09: Record Expenses and Stock Adjustments; 10: Show an explainable Business dashboard; 11: Filter reports and export safe CSV.

**Status:** in-progress

## Comments

- **2026-09-16 inventory at `cd38281`:** the repository has no demo seed command, fixture, credentials, or Audit Event browser. The landing page and README still describe demo access as future work. Existing dashboard and report reads are Business-scoped and Demo-compatible, but a seeded demo must prove every major read workflow against coherent fictional data.
- Shared Demo rejection already exists at `businesses.rules.ensure_business_write_allowed` and the request decorator. Product, membership, Purchase completion/edit/void, Sale completion/edit/void, Expense, and Stock Adjustment services use it with direct-service tests. Business settings plus Purchase and Sale draft creation still persist directly in views, so those mutation entry points have request-only Demo protection and must gain guarded service seams before the ticket can claim complete request-and-service enforcement.
- Existing append-only application events cover Product create/update/deactivate, Staff Member add/deactivate, Purchase complete/void, Sale complete/void, Expense record/void/correct, and manual Stock Adjustment. These are the consequential business, permission, stock, and money lifecycle actions already named by the specification and earlier ticket contracts. Draft Purchase/Sale create/edit, sign-in/password actions, and read activity are deliberately excluded from the audit contract because they do not finalize a stock, money, permission, or immutable business fact. Business creation and Business settings changes are consequential configuration actions and must gain attributed `business.created` and `business.updated` events.
- `AuditEvent` is append-only through the application model/queryset boundary, retains deactivated actors through protected foreign keys, and is already used for the dashboard's recent activity. Ticket 12 does not claim database-trigger-level immutability. The complete browser must retain events from deactivated actors and scope actor choices and results to the current Business.
- Repository-wide guidance remains accurate after this inventory; no `AGENTS.md`, ADR, or module-map ownership change is needed until a new public service/query seam is implemented.
