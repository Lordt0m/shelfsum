# ShelfSum

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Small-shop Owners and Staff Members recording everyday stock and Business activity.

## Product Purpose
Explain Stock on Hand through retained Stock Movements and connect operational reports to recorded Purchases, Sales and Expenses. CONTEXT.md and the accepted implementation specification remain the domain authorities.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Server-rendered Django with progressive TypeScript. Whole-number quantities, backend decimal calculations, Business isolation, role permissions, transactional stock writes, immutable movement history and reversals. The fictional Demo Business is read-only. Estimated Profit is an operational estimate, not accounting, tax or cash profit.

## Brand Commitments
ShelfSum's green identity, welcoming character, warm surfaces and understandable product language, explicitly requested by the user.

## Evidence on Hand
Working catalogue, dashboard, purchase/sale workflows, expenses, adjustments, reports, CSV exports and demo access. No supplied customer testimonials or commercial adoption metrics.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Semantic HTML, keyboard and phone usability, reduced motion and usable JavaScript-free content and navigation.
